Original overlay-pack author's notes (RetroArch setup). Kept for reference —
MinUI reads the viewport from each .cfg's minui_viewport_* keys instead of the
Custom Aspect settings described below.

Gameboy
  Video-Scaling: Integer OFF, Aspect Custom, X 80, Y 13, W 480, H 432,
  Bilinear filtering OFF.
  Palettes are applied per overlay. All need Core Options > GB colorization =
  Internal, then:
    GB_DMG     Internal Palette = Pixelshift - Pack 1
               Pack 1 palette   = Pixelshift 03 - BGB 0.3 Emulator
    GB_Pocket  Internal Palette = TWB 2
               TWB 2 palette    = 117 Silver Shiro
    GB_Bivert  Internal Palette = Pixelshift - Pack 1
               Pack 1 palette   = Pixelshift 27 - GBP Bivert

Gameboy Advance
  Video-Scaling: Integer OFF, Aspect Custom, X 0, Y 0, W 640, H 427,
  Bilinear filtering OFF.
  Suggested with a shader from the interpolation folder, or a normal 2x video
  filter if the device has no shader support.

NeoGeo
  Video-Scaling: Integer OFF, Aspect Custom, X 16, Y 8, W 608, H 448,
  Bilinear filtering optional.
